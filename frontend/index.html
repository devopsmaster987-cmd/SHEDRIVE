How it works

Three steps.
No guesswork.

Designed for a late lecture, a night shift, or a night out. Clear at every stage, with a real person behind the safety net.

  1. 01

    Verify your profile

    Take a selfie, add your phone number, and choose rider or driver. Most accounts clear instantly; the rest go to human review.

    Your account is under review, never "rejected"
  2. 02

    Set your pickup and fare

    Drop a pickup pin, choose a destination, then propose a fare that works for you. Nearby women drivers respond with offers.

    You pick the offer, not an algorithm
  3. 03

    Ride with the city in view

    Follow the car live, keep your emergency contacts one tap away, then pay and rate the trip at the door.

    Live tracking from accept to arrival

Privacy by design

Your face proves you're you.
It never becomes a file.

The selfie you take at signup is processed in memory by our verification service, converted into a numeric embedding, and then discarded. No raw images are kept, logged, or sold. Ever.
