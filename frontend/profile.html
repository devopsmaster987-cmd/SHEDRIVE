Support

Questions, answered

Quick answers about verification, fares and safety.

Why is my account under review?

The face check did not reach a confident match, or the face service was unavailable. A human reviews your account instead of rejecting you automatically.

How does fare negotiation work?

You suggest a fare for your route. Nearby drivers can accept it or send a counter-offer. You choose the offer that works for you.

Who sees my emergency contacts?

They are stored on your account for SOS alerts. They are not shown to drivers or other riders.

How do I change my phone number?

Phone changes need a fresh OTP check, so contact support for now. Keeping the verified number locked protects your account.
